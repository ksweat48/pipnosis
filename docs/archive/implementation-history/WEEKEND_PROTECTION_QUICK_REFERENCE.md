# 🛡️ Weekend Protection - Quick Reference

## What Is It?

Pipnosis automatically closes **ALL open positions** before Friday market close to prevent weekend gap risk.

---

## ⏰ Key Times (EST)

| Time | What Happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Friday 12:00 PM** | ⚠️ Warnings start (hourly) |
| **Friday 2:00 PM** | 🚫 No new trades allowed |
| **Friday 3:00 PM** | 🛡️ All positions auto-closed |
| **Friday 5:00 PM** | 🔒 Market closes |
| **Sunday 5:00 PM** | ✅ Market reopens, normal trading resumes |

---

## Why?

**Gap Risk:** Prices can jump significantly over the weekend when markets are closed. If your position is open, you could lose more than your stop loss.

**Pipnosis Philosophy:** Intraday trading only (1-10 hours max). No overnight or weekend holds.

---

## What You'll See

### 🟡 Warning Phase (Friday 12 PM - 3 PM)
```
⚠️ Weekend approaching: All positions will auto-close in 2h 30m
```
- Yellow banner at top of screen
- Toast notifications every hour
- Countdown timer shows time remaining

### 🟠 Trade Blocking (Friday 2 PM - 5 PM)
```
🛡️ Weekend Protection: No new trades (3h 0m until close)
```
- Orange banner
- New trade attempts are blocked
- AI explains why scanning is disabled

### 🔴 Auto-Closing (Friday 3 PM)
```
🛡️ Weekend Protection: Auto-closing positions now
```
- Red banner
- All open positions close at current market price
- You receive notification with P&L details

### ⚫ Weekend (Sat/Sun)
```
🛡️ Weekend Protection: Market Closed
```
- Gray banner
- No trading activity
- System remains in standby mode

---

## FAQs

**Q: Can I disable this?**
A: No. Weekend protection is mandatory for all users.

**Q: Will I lose my profits?**
A: No! Positions close at current market price. All P&L is credited to your account.

**Q: What if I'm winning big?**
A: Position still closes Friday at 3pm. Better to secure profits than risk weekend gap.

**Q: Can I manually close before 3pm?**
A: Yes! You can always close positions manually at any time.

**Q: What happens to my goal session?**
A: Sessions are marked as completed when positions auto-close. You can start a new goal Sunday evening.

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Plan Ahead:** Don't start new goals after Friday 12pm
2. **Monitor Warnings:** Pay attention to countdown notifications
3. **Manual Close:** Close winning positions early Friday if you prefer
4. **Weekend Break:** Use weekends to review performance and plan next week
5. **Sunday Restart:** Resume trading Sunday 5pm EST when markets reopen
