PROBLEM:
  EURUSD prices (1.158) appearing in XAUUSD chart (should be 4,180+)
  Data "spreading across pairs" when scrolling

ROOT CAUSE:
  Cache saving candles with WRONG symbol due to line:
    const candleSymbol = candle.symbol || symbol;  // ❌ BUG
  
  When switching symbols, old candles retained previous symbol properties,
  causing EURUSD data to be cached under XAUUSD keys.
